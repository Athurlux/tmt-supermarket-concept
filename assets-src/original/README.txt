DIRECTION
Warm cream, deep forest green and vivid orange. Premium neighbourhood supermarket. Rich natural food colours, warm directional sunlight and tactile paper packaging. Orange/green direction informed by public TMT brand references, but generated bag wordmarks are a proposed treatment, not exact official logos.

HERO
Use desktop and mobile images as alternate compositions, not both simultaneously. Keep live HTML headline in left desktop negative space / upper mobile space. Suggested campaign line: FRESH FINDS. FULL BASKETS.
For a genuinely layered GSAP hero, use motion/tmt-grocery-bag-cutout.png on a CSS cream background plus separate product cutouts. Do not overlay another full bag on the baked hero bag. Flat hero scenes provide fallback or alternative compositions.

MOTION
Products are separate assets suitable for transform/opacity entrance choreography, restrained orbit/drift and scroll transitions. Bag cutout is a single flattened bag-plus-groceries object; its contents are not individually separable layers. Avoid huge texture downloads, too many continuous animations, scroll hijacking or unreadable moving controls. Reduced motion and mobile simplification required later.

CATALOGUE
Seven standalone product images: orange, avocado, baguette, bananas, tomatoes, milk and cooking oil. Image groups are not declarations of sell units or weights. These are illustrative generic products, not a verified TMT inventory. Department photos are category imagery, not individual SKU packshots. No real pricing or availability is established.

QUALITY
Transparent PNG edges can contain coloured fringes, particularly the bag and milk bottle. Inspect against final backgrounds and clean or integrate appropriately. Originals have not been retouched or web-optimized. Generate responsive derivatives during implementation. Images are not finished website screens or functional UI.

CONCEPT BOUNDARIES
All imagery is AI-generated concept art. Packaging and delivery scene are fictional; do not describe depicted people as actual staff. No branch photographs or official logo files are included. Use verified photographs for real branches later; do not pass generated interiors off as real premises. Preserve an independent-concept disclosure.
